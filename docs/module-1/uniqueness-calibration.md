`old-formula score range` is `min(mean_distance / 0.5, 1) × 100` applied to
each business's *own* mean distance to its cohort — the actual score the old
`semanticsScore` would have handed the least- and most-distinct business in
the category. (The table in `00-index.md` runs the same formula over the
min/max *pairwise* distances instead, so its range is wider; both show the
same compression.) This is the metric the plan replaces with a percentile rank.

## Verdict

**Compression confirmed.** The most distinct business in any category scores only **28.3** against its own cohort under the old formula. Same-domain profiles sit in a narrow distance band this encoder cannot widen (see `00-index.md` → *CORRECTION — the E5 prefix is not the cause*), so a percentile rank against the cohort's own distribution — Task 6 — is doing 100% of the interpretive work. Nothing downstream may assume the raw score spread is usable.
